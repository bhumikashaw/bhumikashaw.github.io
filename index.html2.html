02 · Flagship Build

Investment Decision Support System

A Streamlit dashboard that benchmarks ~4,200 NSE & BSE-listed companies against their sector the way an analyst would — with a classifier backing the call at scale, not replacing the benchmarking.

  • Calculates WACC, ROCE, Cost of Equity, Cost of Debt and Spread directly from raw company fundamentals — the same inputs an equity analyst would pull first.
  • Benchmarks every metric against sector-relative quartiles (25/50/25), grounded in Fama–French and EVA theory rather than flat cutoffs.
  • Cross-references Damodaran's global WACC-by-sector data as an external benchmark, via a 78-sector mapping built against the underlying dataset.
  • Classifies each company Attractive / Neutral / Risky with a cross-validated Random Forest — a scale check on the benchmarking, not the headline.
  • Visualises results with a hand-built metric bar, so a number is never shown without the sector context around it.
